# Homepage research photographs

These four images replaced the generated research illustrations on 8 September 2026. All show real experiments or hardware from the lab's papers and author project materials. No generated imagery, content replacement or retouching was used. Photo credits on the homepage link to the original source.

The eye-tracking still remains 480 × 320 without upscaling. All are encoded as WebP. Natural colors and photographic content are preserved.
