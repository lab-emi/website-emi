We are thrilled to announce that our EMI Research Group’s brilliant students have achieved remarkable success at this year’s flagship IEEE conferences!

A huge congratulations to our PhD students, **Ang Li**, **Yizhuo Wu, Yi Zhu (Ampleon)**, and MSc students **Sjoerd Groot,** **Huanqiang Duan**, **Haolin Wu**, **Manno Versluis**, and **Kun Qian** for their exceptional work.

- **DeltaDPD: Exploiting Dynamic Temporal Sparsity in Recurrent Neural Networks for Energy-Efficient Wideband Digital Predistortion** **(Y. Wu,** **Y. Zhu, K. Qian****)** was accepted to the 2025 IEEE MTT-S International Microwave Symposium (IMS) and recognized as one of the IMS Top 50 Papers, with an invitation to present at IEEE MWTL. This work is a collaboration between EMI and [**Ampleon Netherlands B.V.**](https://www.ampleon.com/)
- **TCN-DPD: Parameter-Efficient Temporal Convolutional Networks for Wideband Digital Predistortion** **(by H. Duan)** also earned acceptance at IMS 2025.
- At the 2025 IEEE International Symposium on Circuits and Systems (ISCAS), our team’s **DPD-NeuralEngine: A 22-nm 6.6-TOPS/W/mm² Recurrent Neural Network Accelerator for Wideband Power Amplifier Digital Pre-Distortion** **(by A. Li, H. Wu with equal contribution),** supported by [**Global****Foundries**](https://gf.com/) and **CleanUMamba: A Compact Mamba Network for Speech Denoising using Channel Pruning (by S. Groot)** were accepted.

These achievements underscore the innovation, dedication, and impact of our research. Join us in congratulating our students for advancing the state-of-the-art in digital predistortion and speech denoising. We are immensely proud of your hard work and success!
