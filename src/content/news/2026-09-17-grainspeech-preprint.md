Our new arXiv preprint, [**GrainSpeech: Less Context, More Detail for Compact Speech Synthesis**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18856), by **Zitao Liang and Chang Gao**, is now available.

GrainSpeech combines a fixed-receptive-field convolutional encoder with Mel-spectrogram supervision designed to preserve fine acoustic detail. The acoustic model has just **264.8K parameters**, supporting our work on efficient speech synthesis for embedded systems.

Explore the [**GrainSpeech repository**](https://github.com/lab-emi/GrainSpeech) for the code, pretrained checkpoint, example spectrograms, audio demos and instructions.
