1️⃣[**Full-Custom Digital ASIC Design for Edge AI Computing**](https://careers.tudelft.nl/job/Delft-PhD-Position-Full-Custom-Digital-ASIC-Design-for-Edge-AI-Computing-2628-CD/816395202/)

2️⃣ [**Algorithm-Hardware Co-Design for AI-Enhanced Transceivers**](https://careers.tudelft.nl/job/Delft-PhD-Position-Algorithm-Hardware-Co-Design-for-AI-Enhanced-Transceivers-2628-CD/816394402/)

Revolutionize wireless systems (5G/6G, automotive) using AI for power amplifier linearization and interference cancellation. Collaborate with NXP Semiconductors ([Robert van Veldhoven](https://www.linkedin.com/in/robert-van-veldhoven-7306366b/) & [Maarten Molendijk](https://www.linkedin.com/in/maarten-molendijk/)). Requires knowledge of neural networks, signal processing (Python/MATLAB), and RF systems.



**We offer:**

- 4-year full funding (€2,901–€3,707/month)
- Access to TU Delft/NXP facilities and industry networks
- Mentorship in a dynamic, interdisciplinary team



Apply by April 25, 2025

Submit your CV, transcripts, thesis, and references to Dr. Chang Gao: [chang.gao@tudelft.nl](mailto:chang.gao@tudelft.nl)
