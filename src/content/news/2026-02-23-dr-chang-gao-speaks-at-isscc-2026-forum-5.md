On February 19, 2026, Chang Gao gave a featured talk at ISSCC titled "Open-Source AI for Analog Correction: From RF Power Amplifiers to Energy-Efficient Silicon". Speaking in the "Analog for AI and AI for Analog" forum, he discussed the OpenDPD framework and the role of open-source AI in bridging the gap between algorithm design and silicon deployment for analog/RF systems.
