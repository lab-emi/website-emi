We were honored to host **Dr. Chris Dick** at our EMI lab at Delft University of Technology to share and discuss our recent work in radio-frequency digital predistortion (DPD) and to hear an insightful talk that provided an overview of NVIDIA platforms and tools for 5G Advanced and 6G research. We are also happy that our [**OpenDPDv2**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.06849) work, conducted by my PhD students **Yizhuo Wu** and **Ang Li**, came up during his presentation and discussion. We thank Chris for taking the time to visit us despite his busy schedule and wish him a successful industry keynote speech at ICASSP 2026 on May 8.
